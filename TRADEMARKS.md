# littrace trademark policy

> This document is a project policy, not legal advice.

`littrace` is the name of this project. This policy explains what you may do
with the name. It is short on purpose, and it is not a threat.

## The one thing worth understanding

**An open-source licence covers copyright. It does not cover trademarks.**

The [AGPL-3.0-only licence](LICENSE) gives you broad rights to use, study,
modify and redistribute the software. It does not give you permission to use
the littrace name in a way that suggests your build is the official one, or
that the project endorses it. The licence keeps the software free; the name
lets people tell where a build came from.

## What you can do without asking

- **Say what your software is.** "Works with Zotero", "based on littrace",
  "a fork of littrace", "compatible with littrace" — all fine. Describing a
  factual relationship is normal and expected.
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under their own name.
- **Write about littrace.** Articles, tutorials, videos, papers, talks and
  reviews are fine, favourable or not. Screenshots of the interface are fine.
- **Cite it in academic work.**
- **Use the name in a repository or package name that clearly reads as
  third-party**, for example `littrace-tools` or `awesome-littrace`.

## What needs care

- **Modified builds.** The AGPL-3.0-only licence permits you to distribute a modified littrace —
  please do. But do not present it as the official littrace. Give it its own
  name and say what it is based on. "MyLab littrace, based on littrace" is
  fine; shipping a modified build as plain "littrace" is not, because users can
  no longer tell whose build they are running.
- **Anything implying official status.** Do not describe an unofficial build as
  an official release, distribution or product, and do not imply endorsement,
  affiliation or certification that does not exist.
- **A logo, if one is added later.** There are no brand assets today. Do not
  invent an `assets/brand/` directory or treat an icon from the interface as a
  project logo. If a logo is added, this section will be extended to cover it.
- **Domains, app store listings and social accounts** whose name would read as
  the official project. Ask first.

## What is not allowed

- Presenting a modified or third-party build as an official littrace release.
- Claiming endorsement, partnership, affiliation or certification that does
  not exist.
- Registering `littrace`, or a confusingly similar mark, as your own trademark.
- Using the name for something misleading, or in a way that suggests the
  project stands behind it when it does not.

## Forks

Forking is a right the AGPL-3.0-only licence grants you, and this policy does not discourage it.

A fork may say, prominently, that it is based on, derived from, or a fork of
littrace. That is a factual statement about origin and needs no permission.
What a fork should not do is occupy the name: do not call the project itself
`littrace`, do not publish it as an official release channel, and do not use an
official logo as the fork's own identity. Pick a name of your own and credit
littrace in the description.

## Third-party marks

**Zotero** is a trademark of the Corporation for Digital Scholarship. A future
Zotero plugin from this project must say that it is **unofficial and not
affiliated with or endorsed by** the Zotero project or its owners.

**PyMuPDF** and **Artifex**, **DeepSeek**, **Ollama**, **GLM** / **Zhipu** and
**OpenAlex** are the marks of their respective owners. They are used here only
to identify a service, format or dependency. This project is not affiliated
with or endorsed by any of them.

## Registration status

`littrace` is an **unregistered mark**. The registered-trademark symbol is not
used anywhere in this repository, and must not be used, because it asserts a
registration that does not exist. There is no application, serial number or
registration number to cite.

## Ownership and questions

The littrace name is used by the littrace maintainer. No company is involved.
If a use is not covered here, open an issue at
<https://github.com/kingside33/littrace/issues> and ask. This project has no
trademark e-mail address. Most reasonable uses are fine; if in doubt, ask
first.
